# Richer chord decoding and class-aware instruments — 31 October 2026

## Executive summary
A fixed181-label root-first chord decoder improves extension-aware full-pitch error from 59.09% to 51.42% on reused guitar development data, but quality remains above target. Class-aware instrument thresholds and a newly trained15class quality readout fail to beat simpler controls on the primary recording-failure endpoint. Research candidates remain unapplied; broad independent sub-30% performance is unconfirmed.

## Recommendations and actual disposition
- Reject class-aware threshold and richer learned-head default changes; keep the simpler global instrument candidate experimental.
- Retain fixed richer root-first decoding as a research candidate, not below30% quality success or an adopted API. Before larger training, prioritize broader whole-song error analysis and qualify fragmentation/unknown-output behavior.
