# Recognizer Research — methods and claim boundaries

Updated 5 November 2026. Current results are summarized by application area on the [overview](index.html); the [archive](archive.html) retains every milestone, finding and execution checkpoint. [Earlier dated methodological amendments](METHODS-HISTORY.md) are retained verbatim as history, not current permissions.

## What counts as evidence
- Distinguish proposal/source readiness, engineering feasibility, exposed-development performance, independent confirmation and actual-application adoption. Tests, expert reviews, oracle ceilings and successful deployments are not musical gains.
- Use original manual human targets matching each endpoint. Presence, predominant instrument, pitch/activity and lead-versus-accompaniment are different tasks. Model predictions may be features, never ground truth; absent/partial labels remain unknown.
- Group related artists/recordings/compositions and disclose source/publisher/checkpoint selection exposure. Artist-held-out testing on previously examined examples is development, not untouched confirmation. Different-endpoint stress tests are contrary evidence, not equivalent validation.

## Scoring and comparisons
- Headline recording reliability is the fraction above a prospectively declared task tolerance, with severe, missing/invalid, coverage and paired regressions/rescues separately reported. Do not confuse duration error with recording-failure incidence, or a below30% failure goal with a universal per-case30% tolerance.
- Tempo: strict relative BPM deviation>8%, severe>50%; missing is separately included in the combined operational rate. Half/double-rate forgiveness, beat/downbeat timing and literal meter are separate diagnostics.
- Chords: prioritize correct roots; use explicitly declared inversion-forgiven strict-triad and separate full-quality/bass-sensitive metrics. Preserve N versus unknown/X/reference gaps. The latest7-case result remains exposed development with3/7triad failures, not a broad target success.
- Presence: score only known labels, retain intended/scorable/invalid/missing accounting, distinguish installed fusion from saved research comparators, and preserve the historical any-error versus later above-tolerance endpoints.
- Lead routing: compare strongest single, fixed blend and training-only audio-blind/identity priors. Truth-dependent oracle potential is not achieved routing. The selected-three oracle and wider partial-expert union differ. Keep complete accepted-reference sets; no truth/IDs in inference features.

## Execution and adoption gates
- Screen credible task-fit and contrary evidence on a small decisive subset before substantial effort or annotation. Respect rejected-family no-retry rules. A new source or cheap runtime alone does not justify a run.
- Human labeling is justified only by a named candidate/decision; the generic30clip request is paused. Preserve unknown/ambiguous/unsupported cases. No paid annotation/data/compute services.
- Check rights separately for code, weights, annotations and recordings. Current private/noncommercial terms do not clear future commercial/hosted/redistributed use.
- Keep research artifacts separate from the application. Training/tuning and application changes have different authorization boundaries; historical budgets do not renew themselves.
- Require meaningful paired performance AND reliability evidence, independent exact-output reconstruction and explicit integration permission; after integration test actual-app off/on on additional varied labeled data, with category support and broad final scope stated honestly.
