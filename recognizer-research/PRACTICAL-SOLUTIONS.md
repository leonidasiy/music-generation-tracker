The fixed harmonic-emphasis corpus follow-up is deferred before inference, with its preparatory artifacts retained. This is a priority change, not a new accuracy gain or production adoption. ## Active priorities
- Inspect a bounded shortlist of task-specific released recognizers or demonstrated code-level bottleneck fixes.
- Verify actual inference code, obtainable checkpoints, model terms, output semantics and local-runtime feasibility rather than rely on paper headlines or demos.
- Prefer an operational adapter and actual waveform inference over repeated pooling, threshold, small-head or preprocessing variants that have not earned further investment.
- Compare a feasible replacement against the strongest retained control; keep roots, chord quality, missing outputs and regressions visible.
- Carry a verified practical gain into an explicitly scoped application-integration task, rather than stopping at a research report.

## What this does not change
Retained experiment metrics are not rewritten. Existing narrow or exposed recordings remain development diagnostics, not broad independent confirmation. No paid services, unreviewed production edits, or new model-performance claims are authorized by this page.

## Contradictions and gaps
A paper-reported gain is not proof that its released checkpoint will improve this application. Candidate availability, terms and local runtime must be resolved before substantial model acquisition/execution. Negative results reject tested methods, not all preprocessing, training or future recognition systems.

## Next executable deliverable
Select the strongest feasible practical candidate, implement its isolated adapter and perform actual inference, then a useful matched accuracy comparison. The deferred HPSS experiment is not the next automatic task.
